## What is this

Two weeks on a rented bike, starting in Seoul and following the east coast
south, then cutting inland through the mountains before looping back. Not
a guided tour - mostly planned day by day.

## Highlights

Coastal roads early on were the easy part. The mountain passes further
south were the actual reason to go - narrow, quiet, occasionally gravel
where the map didn't warn about it.
