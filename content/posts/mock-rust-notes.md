This is a mock post, kept intentionally short to check how a leaner card
reads next to a longer one on the blog grid.

Picked Rust over Python for this specifically because the compiler forces
correctness at the type level - no silent off-by-one on a linked list, no
implicit copy hiding a bug. Slower to write, faster to trust.

Working through the classics again: linked lists, binary trees, a small
graph traversal exercise. Nothing novel, just rebuilding the muscle.
